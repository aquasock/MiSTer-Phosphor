    Output requirements and privacy

    Inputs must be native FLAC, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo, with CD-sector-aligned lengths. The output contains a standard embedded CUESHEET and SEEKTABLE understood by MiSTer_MP3. Conservative circular mode removes exact zero-valued sectors. Tight mode also removes consecutive outer sectors below −50 dBFS RMS and therefore intentionally trims quiet fade material. Partial sectors are always retained. Audio is decoded and losslessly re-encoded locally; no files are uploaded.
